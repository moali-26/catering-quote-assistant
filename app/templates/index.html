How a quote comes together

  1. Describe your event

    Date, headcount, delivery or pickup, and any dietary needs. The assistant asks about anything missing before it prices.

  2. Get an itemized draft

    Every line uses the menu’s price. Delivery and tax are calculated from the caterer’s rules, so the total adds up.

  3. Owner reviews, you confirm

    The owner checks portions and allergens, then approves. You accept or decline from My Quotes, whenever suits you.
